# Script Templates — Making Sense

Tone reminder: serious facts, deadpan delivery. One joke per beat, never at the expense of accuracy.

## 1. Short video (30–60 seconds)

```
[HOOK ≤3s]  The most important fact, no empty teaser.
  e.g. "[Company] just released [product]. Here's what actually changed."

[WHAT HAPPENED]  1–2 sentences. Who, what, when.

[EVIDENCE]  Key number/feature + who is claiming it.
  Show the source on screen. Label: company claim / research result / reported by media.
  (Joke slot: deflate the hype, not the fact.)

[WHAT IT MEANS FOR YOU]  1–2 sentences. Who's affected and how.

[WHAT WE DON'T KNOW]  1 honest sentence.

[SIGN-OFF]  "Sources are in the caption. This was Making Sense." (+ optional dry closer)
```

Length: about 110–150 words for 45 seconds of narration.

## 2. Carousel

1. **Factual title** (≤12 words) + confidence badge
2. Short context
3. What was announced/found
4. The key number/evidence
5. What we don't know yet
6. What it means for you
7. Sources (numbered) + access date

## 3. Weekly Recap

```
Making Sense Recap — Week of [date]

1. [Factual headline] [✅/🟡]
   [1–2 sentence core] · Source: [publisher]
2. ...
(max 5 stories)

Hoax of the week: [viral claim that didn't hold up, 1–2 lines, with verdict]
```

## 4. Fact-check / Debunk

```
CLAIM: "[quote the viral claim]"
SPOTTED ON: [platform/date]
VERDICT: True | Partly true | Misleading | False | Unproven

ORIGIN: [where the claim came from]
FACTS: [what the primary source says]
WHY IT MISLEADS: [the mechanism of the mistake]
CHECK IT YOURSELF: [short steps]
SOURCES: [list]
```

## 5. "Making Sense of…" explainer

1. The question being answered (1 sentence)
2. The 15-second answer
3. Full explanation with an everyday analogy
4. A real example + source
5. The common misconceptions
6. What this means / practical steps
7. Sources

## 6. Caption

```
[One-sentence factual headline]

[2–3 sentences: summary + what it means]

Status: ✅ Confirmed / 🟡 Reported
Sources:
- ...

#MakingSense #AINews #AI
```

## Approved hook patterns

- "[Entity] just [did X]. Here's what that actually means."
- "A claim is going around that [X]. Here are the facts."
- "Three things actually changed in [product]. One of them is the name."
- "Before you believe [viral claim], check this."

## Before / after

| Before (rejected) | After (on brand) |
|---|---|
| "SHOCKING! This AI just ended programmers' careers!" | "[Company] released a tool that writes code from a description. Their claim: [X]. No independent evaluation yet. Programmers remain, as far as we can tell, employed." |
| "GPT-6 LEAKED, you MUST see this" | "There's a rumor about a new model. No official confirmation, so here's what we can verify, and what we can only squint at." |
| "AI is now smarter than humans!!!" | "A model scored 90% on a benchmark its own maker designed. We'll call that 'promising' and 'a bit like grading your own homework.'" |

## Humor safety quick check

- Does the joke target hype, marketing, or an idea? ✅
- Does it target a person, group, or victim? ❌ cut it.
- Does it change or blur a fact, number, or quote? ❌ cut it.
- Is the story about harm, loss, or safety? ❌ no jokes, play it straight.
